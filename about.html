Hall of Fame & Core Profile

  • Global Distinction: Inducted into the Transglobal World Music Hall of Fame (2025).
  • Artistic Profile: Acclaimed global master of the pipa (Chinese lute), visionary composer, improviser, and educator.
  • Classical Pedigree: Alumna of the Central Conservatory of Music, studying under master Lin Shicheng.

Prestigious Worldwide Venues

  • North America: Carnegie Hall, Lincoln Center Festival, San Francisco Jazz Festival, and the Smithsonian.
  • International Stages: Teatro Colón (Buenos Aires), and festivals in Paris, Milan, Perth, Lisbon, and Spain.
  • Cultural First: First Chinese musician to perform the National Anthem on a pipa at an NBA game.

Orchestral Collaborations

  • International Icons: The Royal Philharmonic Orchestra, South Netherlands Philharmonic, and Heidelberg Philharmonic.
  • Premier U.S. Symphonies: Minnesota Orchestra, San Francisco Symphony, and Saint Paul Chamber Orchestra.

Composition & Commissions

  • Symphonic Works: Created pieces for the Minnesota Orchestra and American Composers Forum.
  • Ensemble Pieces: Commissioned by "The President's Own" United States Marine Band.
  • Abbey Road History: Recorded four composed concerti with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra (2024).

Award-Winning Discography

  • Symphony of Self (2026): Released by Naxos World; won two Global Music Award Gold Medals.
  • Cross-Cultural Collaborations: Captured 13 collective Gold Medals working with flamenco, kora, and oud masters.
  • Global Distribution: Author of the world's first English-language pipa method book via Hal Leonard.

Fellowships & Civic Honors

  • "Gao Hong Day": Proclaimed by St. Paul Mayor Melvin Carter on April 3, 2022, honoring her milestone concert.
  • Elite Art Fellowships: Recipient of a Bush Artist Fellowship and five McKnight Artist Fellowships.

Academic & Leadership Roles

Faculty member at Carleton College directing the Chinese Music Ensemble; Guest Professor at the Central Conservatory of Music. Former Governor of the Recording Academy's Chicago Chapter and Board Member for the American Composers Forum.
